## What it is

A single-piece-looking tray printed as **two mirrored halves** with a **centre
half-lap** on floor, back wall, flange and front lip (floor splits in thickness,
walls split across thickness; the halves slide together, then glue). Open front
(15 mm lip) so equipment and cables are reachable; walls on back + both sides up
to a 25 mm **mounting band** with #6 screws (4 back + 2 per side = **8**).

- organic cable/vent openings: one 90 × 30 stadium port per half in the back
  wall + 2 round Ø14 vents, one 60 × 26 stadium + Ø14 per side wall, three
  36 × 12 band slots per half on back and sides, and two Ø18 semicircle notches
  in the front lip;
- **magnetic front lip:** four Ø6.3 × 3.2 mm pockets (x ±40 and ±110) on the
  front lip face for Ø6 × 3 mm disc magnets — superglue or JB Weld them flush.
  If you want a removable front cover later, mirror this pattern on the cover
  (countersunk for polarity) and glue its magnets facing the matching poles;
- **styling (v1.1):** outer wall corners rounded r7 and inner wall corners
  filled r4 (boolean rounding — print-friendly vertical curves); keeps the boxy
  frame but reads noticeably sleeker in hand;
- eight inside corner gussets (geometry carries the load and limits nylon
  creep);
- heat: the top is open toward the desk only 25 mm — the band slots and wall
  ports are the vent path; keep clearances (do not block the front).

## Print orientation

**Open side up** (floor on the bed) — floor is flat, walls rise, ports print
cleanly. Supports under the top flanges only (the back flange overhangs 18 mm at
~80 mm height; the flange's top face is the desk contact face — keep it flat).
Both halves fit a 220 mm bed with ~3 mm margin per side (210+ beds: adjust
`SHELF_W` / `SHELF_FLANGE_W`).

The lap is nominal fit; if printing the halves in different materials/models,
add 0.2 mm clearance on the lap faces. Glue all mating faces (epoxy), clamp;
optional M4 clamp screws can be drilled at assembly if preferred over glue.

## Material + print settings

- **PA612-CF preferred, PETG-CF fallback** (PLA not recommended: the shelf lives
  in warm, loaded service; nylon absorbs moisture, so dry it; treat ribs/gussets
  as the load path, not the material).
- FDM, ~6 perimeters/walls, 20–30 % gyroid infill, 0.2–0.25 mm layers
  (perimeters carry the load, gyroid is the core).
- Layer-line strength: floor layers are flat (bending across the span is
  layer-parallel, good); walls are vertical (handled by the gussets and the
  mounting band).
- Estimated material: **~0.75–1.0 kg** of filament at those settings
  (solid volume ≈ 720 cm³ across the two halves).

## Assembly

1. Slide the two halves together (half-lap), glue, clamp (or drill + M4).
2. Dry-fit under the desk, mark the 8 holes, epoxy the flanges, screw up.
   Open front faces the user.
3. Re-check: nothing protrudes above the desk; flange tops sit flush (they are
   the contact faces).

## Open items

- Cord-opening count/placement — adjust the stadium/vent loops in
  `desk_mounts.py`; positions are parameters-in-code.
- Print partner check: bed ≥ 215 mm (220+ recommended), nylon drying capability;
  PA612-CF shrinks less than unfilled PA but expect ~0.3–0.5 % — the laps are the
  only fit-critical feature and are glue-filled.
- The shelf is a two-piece print; the half-lap fit is nominal. Print a lap
  coupon before committing the full shelf if the printer is new.
